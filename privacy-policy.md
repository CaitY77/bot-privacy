# Cahit Music — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 13, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Cahit Music is a Discord music bot that allows users to play and manage music in Discord voice channels using slash commands and prefix commands.

This Privacy Policy explains how Cahit Music processes user information.

## 2. Information We Process
Depending on the features used, Cahit Music may process:

- Discord User IDs
- Discord Server IDs
- Discord Channel IDs
- Music commands and song requests
- Message content necessary to process prefix commands
- Music queue and playback information

## 3. How We Use Information
Information is processed to:

- Execute music commands
- Manage music playback and queues
- Maintain bot functionality
- Diagnose technical issues

## 4. Data Storage
Information required for active music sessions may be processed temporarily. Any additional storage, including logs or server settings, will depend on the bot's implementation.

## 5. Data Sharing
Cahit Music does not sell user information. Information may be transmitted to service providers when necessary to deliver music functionality.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion
Users may request information about personal data stored by Cahit Music and request its deletion, subject to applicable requirements.

## 7. Security
Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed by Cahit Music.

## 8. Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the bot or its data practices.
